## Notes for ChatGPT

1. **Stop Loss vs Risk Amount**: These are independent fields. Stop loss is the chart-based technical level where the trade thesis is invalidated. Risk amount is the planned/account-based dollar risk (e.g., 5% of account). They may not always match perfectly -- that's valuable data.

2. **Setup Quality (not Confidence)**: Rate the technical quality of the setup (1-5), not your emotional state. This helps analyze whether better setups correlate with better outcomes.

3. **Structure Break Before Exit**: Answer "yes" if price structure clearly broke before you exited, "no" if it held, or "unsure" if unclear. This distinguishes emotional exits from technically justified ones.

4. **Would Take Again**: "withAdjustment" means you'd take a similar setup but with specific changes to execution or management.

5. **What Worked / What Failed**: These use predefined tags. Choose the closest matches. If none fit perfectly, describe briefly.
